Privacy Policy

Tolong Saja (“we”, “us”) · Last updated: 16 September 2026

Contact: support@tolongsaja.app

1. Who we are

Tolong Saja is a local task marketplace app for connecting people who need help with people who can help (currently focused on the Klang Valley / Malaysia).

2. Data we collect

  • Account: email (via Google / Apple sign-in), display name, bio, avatar, preferred language.
  • Tasks & content: task titles, descriptions, photos, location name / coordinates you choose to attach, chat messages, reviews, ratings.
  • Device: push notification token; approximate or precise location when you grant permission (to show nearby tasks / fill location).
  • Safety: user reports (reason + optional notes) used for moderation / auto-ban after repeated reports.

3. How we use data

  • Provide core marketplace, chat, and review features.
  • Send transactional push notifications (new messages, task updates, reviews).
  • Enforce safety rules (reports, bans).
  • Operate and improve the service (reliability, abuse prevention).

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Sharing

We use processors such as Supabase (database/auth/storage), Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging (push), and Google / Apple (sign-in). Content you post (tasks, reviews, profile) may be visible to other users as part of the product.

5. Retention

Chat messages are deleted after about 30 days. Account and task-related data are kept while your account is active, and as needed for safety / legal obligations after deletion requests.

6. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke location, camera, photos, or notification permissions in system settings.
  • Update or remove profile fields in-app.
  • Request account / data deletion by emailing support@tolongsaja.app.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the service if you are under the age required by local law.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, in-app notice.
